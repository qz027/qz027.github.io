Qianyi Zhang is a New York City-based architectural designer and researcher working in affordable housing. With a dual MS/M.Arch from the University of Washington and a BFA in Industrial Design from RISD, her work has a strong focus on environmental sustainability, material circularity, and social justice in the built environment.

Her research on building material supply chains has been recognized by the ACSA Design for Freedom Competition and presented at the USGBC Building Transformation Forum.

As a Certified Passive House Consultant, Qianyi utilizes energy modeling tools to identify retrofit opportunities and evaluate design options.

Currently accepting freelance energy modeling projects for homeowners. Thinking about retrofitting your home to improve energy efficiency? Or are you building a house and would like to make design decisions that improve comfort and building performance? Qianyi can help. Email to connect.
